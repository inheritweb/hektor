---
title: Enter an organisation workspace
description: Select a tenant and understand the access checks Hektor applies.
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

1. Sign in to Hektor once with your normal account.
2. Open the account switcher at the bottom of the application menu.
3. Select the organisation you want to administer.

Hektor remembers the selected organisation in this browser. Every organisation-scoped request is checked against both your authenticated account and your active membership. Selecting an organisation does not grant access by itself.

If you are a platform administrator entering an organisation, Hektor records that as platform access. It does not create or imitate an organisation membership.

To leave the organisation workspace, select **Personal account** in the account switcher.
